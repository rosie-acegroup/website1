Our Story

Where Strong Beginnings
Take Root.

The Palisades welcomes PJECC back, stronger than ever.

Over 20 years ago, Chabad of the Pacific Palisades opened PJECC with a simple vision: a place where children could grow in an environment filled with warmth, curiosity, beauty, and genuine care. As our school grew, so did that vision, slowly shaping a campus and classrooms that feel alive with childhood.

After the Palisades fires forced a temporary move to the Westside, what began as a crisis response grew into an expansion. Today we're entering a new chapter: reopening our Palisades campus while continuing to nurture our Westside community, serving both and welcoming new families.

Through it all, our purpose has stayed the same: to create a place where every child is known, every family feels welcome, and childhood is celebrated in all the ways that matter most.

Why PJECC

A Rare Kind of Joyful.

Children aren't just cared for or just taught, they're genuinely guided to become thinkers, explorers, and confident individuals. Here's what makes childhood at PJECC different.

Reggio-Inspired

Children are the driving force behind what they learn, engaging in collaborative, hands-on, joyful discovery that builds curiosity and resilience.

Rooted in Nature

One of the few outdoor Jewish preschools by the coast, a natural landscape of grass fields, gardens, and play built by the hands of our community.

Jewish Values, Woven In

Judaism lives naturally in the rhythm of each day, nurturing kindness, respect, and a joyful connection to heritage, with every family welcomed.

Small Classes, Low Ratios

Small classes with low student-to-teacher ratios, led by specialists in early childhood development who know each child deeply.

Play-Based & Joyful

Children learn best when they're having fun. We honor childhood rather than rush it, inspiring wonder through play, art, music, and exploration.

A Real Community

We invest as deeply in our teachers and families as we do in our children. It's not just a program. It's a place that feels like family.

Our Classes

Where Your Child's
Story Begins.

From first steps to their leap toward kindergarten, every class meets children exactly where they are, with warmth, wonder, and room to grow.

Chaverim Alef

Infant Center

A warm, loving first home away from home. Through responsive care, sensory play, music, and meaningful relationships, we build trust, connection, and a lifelong love of learning from the very beginning.

Chaverim Bet

Toddlers

Curiosity comes alive. Children explore outdoors, create, climb, and build friendships through purposeful play, growing in confidence, independence, language, and social-emotional skills.

Chaverim Gimmel

2-Year-Olds

Imagination takes center stage. Through open-ended materials, art, storytelling, and dramatic play, children develop creativity, communication, and a joyful love of learning.

Chaverim Daled

3-Year-Olds

Deeper discovery through hands-on investigations inspired by nature, literature, and Jewish traditions, encouraging collaboration, critical thinking, and the confidence to explore new ideas.

Chaverim Hey

4-Year-Olds / TK

Our oldest learners are confident, capable, and ready to lead. Alongside creative, purposeful play, they build a strong foundation in literacy, math, and problem-solving, ready for kindergarten and beyond.

Our Philosophy

Educate a child according to his way.

At PJECC, we believe childhood is meant to be joyful. Every child is seen as an individual, full of curiosity, creativity, and endless potential. Our role is to create an environment where children feel safe to explore, ask questions, build friendships, and discover the world around them. We honor childhood rather than rush it.

Frequently Asked
Questions.

What are your hours and schedule options?

We offer flexible scheduling to fit your family:

  • Half day: 9:00 AM–12:30 PM
  • Full day: 9:00 AM–3:00 PM
  • Extended care: 7:30–9:00 AM & 3:00–5:30 PM
What ages do you serve?

We welcome children from infancy through TK (about 0–5 years), across five age groups, our Chaverim classes:

  • Chaverim Alef: Infant Center
  • Chaverim Bet: Toddlers
  • Chaverim Gimmel: 2-Year-Olds
  • Chaverim Daled: 3-Year-Olds
  • Chaverim Hey: 4-Year-Olds / TK
Where are your two campuses?

We have two coastal locations:

  • Palisades Campus: 17315 Sunset Blvd, Pacific Palisades, CA 90272
  • Westside Campus: 1537 Franklin St, Santa Monica, CA 90404
Do we have to be Jewish to attend?

Not at all. PJECC is open to families of all backgrounds. What unites our families is the desire to raise thoughtful, confident children who feel loved, respected, and connected. Jewish values are woven naturally into each day, nurturing kindness, respect, and belonging for every child.

What is your educational approach?

We're a Reggio-inspired, play-based program, one of the few outdoor Jewish preschools by the coast. Children learn through play, nature, art, and hands-on discovery in small classes with low student-to-teacher ratios, guided by specialists in early childhood development. We honor childhood rather than rush it.

How do I apply or schedule a tour?

The best first step is to come see us. Schedule a tour using the form below, or reach out at hello@pjecc.org. We're now enrolling for the 2026–2027 school year at both campuses.
